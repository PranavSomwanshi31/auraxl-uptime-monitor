Cloudflare / CDN SSL Fix:
1. Log in to your Cloudflare Dashboard.
2. Go to SSL/TLS → Overview.
3. Change encryption mode from "Full (Strict)" to "Full" or "Flexible" so SSL handshakes complete smoothly.
Hostinger / cPanel Server Fix:
1. Log in to your Hosting Control Panel (Hostinger / cPanel).
2. Navigate to Websites → SSL.
3. Click "Reinstall SSL" and ensure "Force HTTPS" is enabled.
